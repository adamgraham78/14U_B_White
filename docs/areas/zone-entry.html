ZE Zone Entry

The triangle carried through the neutral zone with a trailer. Then get to the net.

Blocks
16
Drills
5

Why this, for this team

Entries are where offence actually gets generated at this level — not from sustained cycling. Get in with possession and support, drive the net, and have a shape to fall into if it doesn't produce.

The progression

  1. Week 16Carry & trailerTriangle through the neutral zone. Someone arrives late, behind the puck.
  2. Week 17The readCarry it in or put it in. Speed through the line decides which.
  3. Week 18Get to the netDrive it, shoot it, retrieve it. A rule, not a system — one sentence, no diagrams.
If the net drive doesn't produce, the fallback is the triangle in the O-zone (Weeks 22–23) — a shape to rotate into, not a scramble.

Drill library is thin here — only 5 tagged drills for 16 blocks. Needs sourcing before Week 16.

What to say on the ice

Same words from every coach. The kids should hear one phrase, not four versions of it.

Speed through the line.Somebody's late — be the trailer.Carry it or put it in. Don't drift.Get to the net.

What goes wrong

  • All three entering flat and wide, no trailer, one bad pass from a rush the other way.
  • Dumping it in with nobody skating to retrieve.
  • Stopping at the top of the circles instead of driving through the net.
